# DeckCover

The deck's first slide: a navy gradient, the deck's statement as its title, one subtitle line, and date and basis at the foot.

## Anatomy

1. **Background**: linear gradient 135° from `navy-600` through `navy-700` to `navy-900`. Optionally a faint chart motif from the deck's data on the right (white at 8–20% opacity, dashed `slate-300` outlines), never decoration unrelated to the content.
2. **Deck label**: the deck's short name in `footnote` bold, uppercase, `slate-300`, over a short 4px `slate-300` bar. Not an eyebrow over the title: it sits alone at the top.
3. **Title**: `cover-title`, white, a statement ("Slower growth is the new normal"), never a topic.
4. **Subtitle**: one line in `body` at 28px, `slate-300`: what the deck covers and for whom.
5. **Foot**: date, a `navy-500` vertical bar, the basis ("Based on the IMF World Economic Outlook"). Document status (Draft, Confidential) goes here too. No sentence about sources or method.

The title block fills the height: label at the top, title and subtitle in the middle third, foot at the bottom.
