A skill earns its place only if work goes worse without it.

## Before

Settle with me via /think-it-through whether we need the skills and what they should do, in intent.md, with any rare harm a window can't show. If they never fire or are covered elsewhere, propose retiring now. Else propose one ablation (off, or a part cut) or variant (a part condensed or rewritten) and pre-register it in trial.md: what should go worse or better, how many sessions decide. Keep both outside the skill folders. Once I agree, apply it in every agent, copy recent sessions as baseline and the window's as they come, and schedule the second pass here.

## After

In a fresh session, evaluate the window against the baseline and what we wrote. Ablations and condensing need only be no worse; a rewrite must be better where we said. Count where I corrected or re-explained; switching back is the verdict. Look as hard for needs nobody wrote down. Record in trial.md: keep the change, rebuild only what is needed, or retire; if unclear, retire unless intent.md names a rare harm.
