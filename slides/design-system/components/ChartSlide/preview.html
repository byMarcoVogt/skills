What shaped the record

Base effect. 2021’s 6.7% was reopening, not new capacity.

Rate shock. An energy spike and the fastest rate hikes in decades cut growth to 3.3% by 2023.

Resilience. A strong US and emerging Asia held 2024–25 at 3.5%, despite new tariffs.

Ageing. Shrinking workforces cut trend growth in China and Europe.

So what: plan volumes on world growth near 3%, not on a rebound.
