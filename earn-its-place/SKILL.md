Work with me on one skill or a set of them. A skill earns its place only if work goes worse without it. Find out in two passes, with a window of real work in between.

## Before

Use /think-it-through to find out with me what the skills are for, then write, update or consolidate it into an intent.md. Audit the skills, then propose a test plan for the window. Once I agree, switch them off. The second pass starts in a fresh session, possibly after transcripts have expired. Leave it a trial file, keep copies of the sessions as the window runs, and schedule it on this machine.

## After

When the window ends, look back over it. Find the sessions whose task the skills would have touched and judge their output against what we wrote down. Look just as hard for what we didn't write down: needs the window turned up that neither the skills nor our notes covered. Report both, then propose: rebuild only what is needed, or retire. Too few relevant sessions can mean the window was too short or that I no longer need the skills. Say which you think.
