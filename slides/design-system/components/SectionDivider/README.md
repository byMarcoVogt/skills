# SectionDivider

A full `navy-700` slide that opens a section with its place in the deck and the section's statement.

The example opens section 2 of 4 of a world-economy briefing.

## Anatomy

1. **Canvas**: 1920 × 1080, `navy-700` fill; the house margins (`margin-side` 80, `margin-top` 72, `margin-bottom` 124); content vertically centred.
2. **Place**: "Section 2 of 4" in `footnote` bold, `slate-300`.
3. **Bar**: a short 4px `slate-300` bar, `space-4` below the place and `space-6` above the statement.
4. **Statement**: the section's message in `cover-title` (serif, 104/110, weight 500), white, a full sentence with a verb.
5. **Page number**: bottom right (right 80px, bottom 52px) in `footnote` bold, `slate-300`. No footer rule, no source, no sticker.

For the appendix, the statement is "Appendix" and the place line can be dropped.

## What the consumer provides

The section's number and the total count, the section's statement as one sentence, and the page number.
